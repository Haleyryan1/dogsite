About Haley

Positive methods. Real-life results.

I believe training should build trust between you and your dog, not just compliance. My approach is reward-based and rooted in patience — the goal is a dog who wants to make good choices, and a handler who knows how to guide them there.

Whether you're starting from scratch with a new puppy, working through a specific behavior issue, or just want a more polite walking companion, I'll meet you and your dog where you are.
